By downloading, installing, or using the Application, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Collected Automatically
When you access and use the Application, certain non-personal and technical information may be collected automatically by our systems and third-party SDKs:

* **Device & Hardware Information:** Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, system language, unique device identifiers, screen resolution, and network connection type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G).
* **Advertising Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID) used for delivering, personalizing, and measuring in-app advertisements.
* **Usage & Diagnostic Data:** Application launch times, screen views, feature usage, user interaction metrics, crash logs, error traces, and performance data.
* **Notification Tokens:** Device push tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging / OneSignal) used exclusively to send you app updates, newly trending movies, or technical notifications if you have enabled notifications.

### B. Information You Provide Directly
* **Favorites & Watchlists:** Movies and TV shows added to your favorites or watchlist are stored locally on your device via SQLite / Room Database and are not transmitted to or stored on our external servers.
* **Search Queries:** Search terms entered into the search bar are processed to query public entertainment databases (e.g., TMDb) for relevant titles.
* **Trakt.tv Integration (Optional):** If you choose to connect your Trakt.tv account, authentication is handled directly through Trakt's official OAuth mechanism. We do not store your Trakt password.

### C. Information We DO NOT Collect
We respect your privacy and strictly avoid collecting sensitive personal data. We do **not** collect:
* Precise GPS location data
* Personal identity documents or government IDs
* Contact lists, address book, or phone records
* Camera or microphone recordings
* Payment, credit card, or financial banking credentials
* Photos, media files, or personal storage contents

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:
1. **Application Functionality:** To display movie and TV series metadata, trailers, recommendations, cast information, and trending catalogs.

## 4. Advertising & Your Choices

### Resetting or Disabling Advertising ID (AAID)
You have control over how your advertising identifier is used on Android:
* **Reset Advertising ID:** Go to **Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID**.
* **Delete / Opt-Out of Personalized Ads:** Go to **Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID** (or enable **Opt out of Ads Personalization** on earlier Android versions).

### Notification Preferences
You can enable or disable push notifications at any time:
* Within Android device settings: **Settings > Apps > MovieBox > Notifications**.
* Within the Application: **Settings screen > Push Notifications toggle**.
