Privacy Policy — USB Endoscope Camera
Effective Date: July 17, 2026 Last Updated: July 17, 2026

1. Introduction
Thank you for using USB Endoscope Camera ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Android application. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect
2.1 Information You Provide
The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use its core features.

2.2 Camera & Media Data
The App accesses your device's USB-connected endoscope or borescope camera to display a live video preview.
Photos and videos you choose to capture or record are saved directly to your device's local storage (DCIM/Camera folder).
We do not upload, transmit, or share any images or videos captured through the App to any server or third party.
2.3 Device & USB Permissions
The App requests the following permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

Permission	Purpose
android.permission.CAMERA	Required on certain devices to access the USB camera stream
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO	Required only if your endoscope device has a built-in microphone and you choose to record audio
android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE	To save captured photos and videos to your device gallery
android.permission.USB_PERMISSION (runtime)	To connect to your USB endoscope device
2.4 Automatically Collected Technical Data
The App does not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or usage statistics. No data is transmitted from your device to our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information
Because the App operates entirely on-device and does not transmit data externally, all processing happens locally on your Android device. Specifically:

Live camera preview is rendered locally and is never streamed or recorded without your explicit action.
Captured photos/videos are stored in your device's media library and are accessible only by you and other apps you authorize.
4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal data or media files with third parties.

We may disclose information only in the following circumstances:

Legal compliance: If required by law, regulation, or valid governmental request.
Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.
5. Data Storage and Security
All data generated by the App (photos, videos) is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any remote servers or cloud storage associated with this App.

You are responsible for the security of your device and the media files stored on it. We recommend using your device's built-in security features (screen lock, encryption) to protect your data.

6. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Third-Party Libraries
The App uses open-source libraries to support USB camera functionality (such as the UVC camera library). These libraries operate entirely on-device and do not collect or transmit any data independently. No third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs are included in the App.

8. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

Access: Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
Opt-out: Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to opt out of.
Since the App stores all data locally on your device, you can manage and delete your photos/videos directly through your device's Gallery or Files app.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
